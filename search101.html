Search101

Our method for turning a subject into a clear study plan.

Method v1.1 · Updated 21 Oct 2026

The rules

  1. Confirm the university, course, semester and exam details.
  2. Use the current syllabus and lecture material first. Label older material with its year.
  3. Check book titles and editions before assigning chapters.
  4. Explain important ideas, compare theories and apply them to examples.
  5. Use authorized past assessments to look for patterns when evidence is available. State which years and papers support a pattern.
  6. Write original flashcards and practice questions with explanations.
  7. Label unknowns and suggested priorities clearly. Qualifications or background do not establish an instructor’s exam habits.
  8. Keep sources, update dates and version history so corrections are traceable.

The workflow

Search101: verify the course.
Search102: map topics and chapters.
Search103: examine available past assessments.
Search104: create study tools.
Search105: review mistakes and refine with new evidence.
